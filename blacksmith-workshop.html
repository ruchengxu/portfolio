Blacksmith Workshop

Project Introduction

Blacksmith Workshop is a stylized 3D environment designed as an NPC shop for an RPG, where players could buy and upgrade equipment. My goal is to build an optimized, game-ready model and finish it with PBR textures and rendering.

Workflow

References & Planning

I gathered references for rustic architecture and blacksmith workshop layouts, focusing on a space where players could buy and repair weapons and armor. An anvil and furnace serve as the key workstations, while the open structure allows players to walk in and explore.

Modeling & Refinement

I developed the blockout into a detailed model, refining the timber supports, roof tiles, and brick furnace. I added props such as the anvil, workbench, and barrels to make the workshop feel functional.

Sculpting

I focused on giving the workshop a rugged, handcrafted feel through uneven surfaces, worn edges, and subtle material details. I kept the sculpting restrained to preserve the stylized shapes, while adding enough variation to suggest age and use.

Chimney: I originally planned to create the brickwork using only a texture and bump map, but that would have left the chimney looking too boxy and uniform. I wanted the workshop to feel rugged and slightly run-down, so I broke up the chimney’s silhouette and exposed some of the bricks underneath.

Roof Tiles: Although the goal is a stylized piece, I added subtle stone texture to the roof tiles so they wouldn’t look brand new. I kept the detail restrained to preserve their simple, readable shapes while giving the workshop a weathered feel.
